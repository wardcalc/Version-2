The Genesis of the Project

Transitioning from the classroom to the hospital ward is one of the most jarring experiences in medical training. Time becomes the most expensive currency in the room. During acute presentations—whether calculating a GCS or assessing an APGAR score—there is no time to second-guess memory. Medical professionals need immediate, evidence-based data.

school

Built by a Student

WardCalc is a grassroots clinical tool, meticulously designed by a final-year medical student navigating the very same wards. Every pixel has been stripped of unnecessary friction. There are no splash screens, no forced logins, and no cluttered menus. Just pure, immediate clinical utility optimized for high-stress environments.

language

Multilingual Matrix

Healthcare is inherently global. WardCalc is the first clinical directory built with a seamless multilingual matrix supporting English, German, Russian, and Uzbek. It allows practitioners to calculate scores in their native language during emergencies, while also acting as an ambient educational tool for exams like the German Fachsprachprüfung (FSP).

verified

Uncompromising Accuracy

In medical software, a calculation error is a patient safety event. Every protocol coded into WardCalc is strictly derived from universally accepted, peer-reviewed medical guidelines. Our tools do not simply spit out a raw number; they provide the structured breakdown and clinical interpretation necessary for bedside decisions.

Core Value

Commitment to Accessibility

We believe that tools directly impacting patient mortality should not be restricted by geographic economic disparities. A junior doctor in a resource-limited setting deserves the exact same computational support as an attending in a private institution. The core calculators of WardCalc are, and will remain, 100% free to access.
